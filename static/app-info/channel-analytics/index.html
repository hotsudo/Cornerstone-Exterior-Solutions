Christian YouTube Analytics Collector

A personal reporting tool for its owner’s YouTube channel.

The collector runs in Google Apps Script. With the owner’s authorization, it reads YouTube video metadata and aggregate analytics, creates or reuses a YouTube Reporting job, and imports available reports into a private Google Sheet.

What the collector does

  • Stores daily viewing, watch-time, engagement and subscriber metrics.
  • Retrieves available retention, traffic-source and thumbnail-performance reports.
  • Supports comparisons by work, author, video length and publication timing.

The collector reads channel data. It does not publish, edit or delete videos.

This website provides app information and policy notices only. It has no account registration, sign-in feature or public analytics dataset. No AI service is connected to the collector today.
