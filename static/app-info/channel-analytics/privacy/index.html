Privacy policy

Updated November 7, 2026

Christian YouTube Analytics Collector is an owner-operated personal tool using YouTube API Services, Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. It is maintained by Caleb Otsubo.

Information accessed and purpose

With authorization, the collector reads channel and video identifiers, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnail URLs, publication details, privacy status and video statistics. It retrieves available aggregate reports covering views, watch time, engagement, subscriber changes, retention, traffic sources, search terms, countries, devices, subscription status, and thumbnail impressions and click-through rate.

This information supports the owner’s channel reporting and publishing comparisons. The collector does not retrieve individual viewer identities or comment text, and it does not request Google passwords.

Storage and processing

Report data, classifications and experiment notes are stored in the bound Google Sheet. Operational information is also stored in Apps Script properties, execution logs and report-import records. Google provides the services that process and store this information under its Privacy Policy. This informational website does not receive or display the collected channel data.

Sharing and AI

The collector has no automatic transfer to advertisers, data brokers or AI services. The owner controls spreadsheet sharing and any separate exports or disclosures. A future authenticated AI reader is planned but is not connected today.

Retention and deletion

Historical records and snapshots remain until the owner removes them; current-report tables are refreshed or replaced as described in the Sheet. The collector does not automatically erase stored copies when authorization is revoked.

To stop collection, disable the Apps Script trigger and remove the app’s access through Google Account connections. To remove stored reports, delete the relevant spreadsheet data or the spreadsheet itself, and separately remove copies or exports. Revoking access alone does not erase previously stored information. Google’s retention practices apply to its service logs and backups. Deleting collector records does not delete videos or data held by YouTube.

For assistance with access or deletion, contact caleb@cornerstonecrewmo.com.

This website

This project adds no advertising, tracking scripts, sign-in form or analytics database to these informational pages. The hosting provider, Netlify, may process routine technical request information when serving them. See Netlify’s privacy policy.

Google API Limited Use

Christian YouTube Analytics Collector’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Updates

This notice will be updated before materially changing the collector’s data use or connecting a separate AI reader.
