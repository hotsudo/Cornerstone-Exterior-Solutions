Usage terms

Updated November 7, 2026

This personal tool is intended for its owner’s authorized channel reporting. It is not a public account service or an official Google or YouTube product.

By using the collector, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge the collector’s privacy policy.

Reporting limitations

Reports depend on Google’s APIs and may be delayed, revised, incomplete or unavailable. Calendar-based publication comparisons are not exact first-24-hour measurements. Review the source data, observation periods and reporting limitations before making publishing decisions.

Owner controls

The owner controls authorization, scheduling, spreadsheet access and deletion of stored reports. The collector does not publish or modify channel content.
