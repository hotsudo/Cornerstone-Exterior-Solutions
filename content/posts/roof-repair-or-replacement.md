A roof problem does not always mean you need a complete replacement. The right solution depends on the roof’s age, the extent and location of the damage, and whether the underlying system is still performing as intended.

### A repair may make sense when

- Damage is limited to a small area.
- Most of the roof is still in good condition.
- A flashing, vent, or isolated material failure is causing the issue.
- The roof is not near the end of its expected service life.

### Replacement may be the better value when

- Problems are widespread or keep returning.
- Materials show significant age, curling, cracking, or loss.
- Leaks or moisture have affected multiple areas.
- You are planning other exterior improvements and want a coordinated result.
